# A1C (HbA1c) Test

## What the A1C Test Measures

The A1C is a blood test used to evaluate glucose management in patients with type 2 diabetes and prediabetes. It measures average blood glucose over the past 3 months. Clinicians use the A1C both to establish diabetes diagnoses and to monitor disease control over time.

## Understanding A1C Results

Results are expressed as percentages; higher values indicate higher average blood sugar:

- **Normal:** below 5.7%
- **Prediabetes:** 5.7% to 6.4% (increased risk for type 2 diabetes; annual retesting commonly recommended)
- **Type 2 Diabetes:** 6.5% or higher

For patients already diagnosed with diabetes, testing occurs at least twice yearly. Many patients target an A1C below 7%, though individual goals vary.

## Key Distinction

The A1C differs from daily self-monitoring blood glucose checks: it provides a longitudinal 3-month average rather than a point-in-time measurement.
