# Vitamin B12 Deficiency Anemia

## Overview

Vitamin B12 deficiency anemia occurs when the body does not have enough healthy red blood cells due to insufficient B12. Red blood cells deliver oxygen throughout the body, so adequate B12 levels are essential.

## Causes

B12 comes from meat, poultry, shellfish, eggs, fortified cereals, and dairy. Absorption requires intrinsic factor, a protein released by stomach cells.

Conditions impairing absorption:
- **Pernicious anemia** — body destroys stomach cells producing intrinsic factor.
- GI surgery (especially weight-loss procedures).
- Crohn's disease and celiac disease.
- Prolonged antacid or **metformin** use.
- Nitrous oxide abuse, alcohol use.

Dietary deficiencies: strict vegetarian/vegan diets, poor infant nutrition, inadequate pregnancy nutrition.

## Symptoms

Early symptoms may be absent or mild. Common: diarrhea or constipation, fatigue, lack of energy, lightheadedness, appetite loss, pale skin, irritability, exertional shortness of breath, swollen red tongue or bleeding gums.

Prolonged deficiency causes neurological damage: numbness and tingling of hands and feet, balance loss, concentration difficulties, confusion or mental status changes including dementia. Severe cases: psychosis and hallucinations.

## Diagnosis

Physical exam may reveal reflex or sensation abnormalities. Tests: CBC, reticulocyte count, lactate dehydrogenase, serum bilirubin, **serum vitamin B12 level**, methylmalonic acid, homocysteine. Antibody tests (anti-intrinsic factor, anti-parietal cell) help identify autoimmune causes. EGD or bone marrow biopsy may clarify diagnosis.

## Treatment

Treat underlying cause. For dietary deficiency, oral B12 supplements often suffice. For absorption disorders: B12 injections, often once a month. Severe cases may need frequent initial injections, potentially lifelong. Dietary variety alongside supplementation.

## Prognosis

People with B12-deficiency anemia often do well with treatment. Prompt intervention prevents permanent nerve damage. Severe or prolonged nerve damage may leave lasting effects.
